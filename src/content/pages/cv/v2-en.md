I am a classroom practitioner first, method-builder second, and translator between languages and communities as often as the day requires.

## What I do

I teach Spanish, French, and English in secondary and middle school settings where most students arrive with no shared first language and no assumption that school will be welcoming. I specialise in IB MYP and DP Language Acquisition, with a developed methodology — NeuroSpanish — that ties comprehensible input, embodied learning, and narrative sequence to the daily work of second-language acquisition. I design units, assessments, and resources that keep the learner at the edge of their competence, not behind it. I co-advise and tutor pastoral care because I have learned that the person who arrives matters more than the scorecard they carry.

## How I got here

**Now — Da Nang, Vietnam**  
Developing NeuroSpanish: a proprietary Spanish-acquisition system built from SENSUM scaffolding, story-immersion, and the 4E view of embodied cognition. Building AI-generated comprehensible-input pipelines and interactive resources for grades 6–12. This is where the ideas from my master’s thesis and classroom years are converging into a coherent and teachable practice.

**2025 — UWC Thailand, Phuket**  
Spanish teacher, IB MYP Language Acquisition. Mixed-ability secondary classes; interdisciplinary modules that embedded language in experiential and outdoor education; pastoral co-advising for Grade 6. It taught me that homeroom and language classroom are the same space: the place where a young person tests whether they can be safe while they grow.

**2021 to 2023 — Tarsus American College, Turkey**  
Spanish teacher. Designed and delivered the full secondary curriculum for more than 120 students across two years; authored all unit plans, assessments, and the scope-and-sequence from scratch. Led creation of the region’s first DELE-accredited centre with Instituto Cervantes. Built more than thirty interactive H5P resources and introduced data-driven formative feedback in the LMS. Engagement and turnaround both improved measurably. That period taught me that curriculum is design, and that design is a form of care.

**2020 to 2021 — Colegio Alvernia Bilingüe, Bogotá**  
Communication and Intercultural World Teacher. English for secondary students, plus a cross-curricular project that tied language learning to ecological awareness. Implemented a new LMS assessment architecture that shortened feedback cycles. A reminder that even in one’s own country, teaching is always translation — between languages, between expectations, between what a system promises and what a classroom needs.

**2019 — Vicariato Apostólico de Inírida, Guainía**  
Spanish and English teacher in a remote, multilingual, multilingual community in the Colombian Amazon. Designed a place-based curriculum that wove indigenous linguistic and cultural context into primary and secondary language learning. This is where I learned that comprehensible input is not a technique; it is a respect for the fact that every learner arrives with a world already built.
