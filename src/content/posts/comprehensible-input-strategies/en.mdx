Immersive, context-driven environments are the bedrock of glotodidactics. A robust curriculum does not explicitly explain grammar upfront; instead, it provides an ecosystem of rich, understandable messages.

Drawing inspiration from classical immersion frameworks, the goal is to lower the affective filter and allow the brain's pattern-recognition engines to work naturally. 

However, auditory input alone can sometimes lack physical grounding. To maximize retention, these auditory streams should be paired with the physical frameworks discussed in [[Somatic Enactivism and Multimodal Input]]. The integration of sound and movement stabilizes the learning environment.
