Interactive environments are redefining how we understand linguistic input. Rather than presenting vocabulary in static sheets, we can scaffold comprehension using dynamic components. By embedding multi-sensory media directly into the reading flow, learners activate their spatial memory, grounding abstract words in somatic contexts.

## Somatic Anchors and 360 Environments

Language acquisition happens when input is linked directly to perception. Below, we've embedded an interactive 360-degree panorama. Try dragging the panorama to look around; it represents a spatial trigger where vocabulary can be hidden or discovered:

<ImmersiveViewer image="https://pannellum.org/images/jura.jpg" alt="Interactive 360 Classroom Concept" />

When a student explores an interactive space, their motor control is actively engaged. The cognitive effort shifts from passive reading to active, embodied exploration.
