We must dismantle the instinct to teach language through explicit rules and paradigms. Natural induction is a highly versatile asset designed to guide learners into discovering linguistic architecture organically, through carefully sequenced exposure.

Much like navigating a new physical space, the learner deduces the "rules" of the environment by interacting with it. This requires highly intentional structural progressions in our materials. 

If we map these sequences correctly, the cognitive load is managed beautifully. This architectural approach relies heavily on consistent [[Comprehensible Input Strategies]] to provide the raw materials from which the learner constructs meaning.
