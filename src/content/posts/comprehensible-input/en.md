Language acquisition is not an analytical puzzle of memorizing syntax at a desk. It is a biological necessity, an enactment of reality that happens at the edge of current understanding. To facilitate this in mixed-ability classrooms, we must design context-rich environments.

## The Scaffolding Pipeline

Rather than relying on direct translation, we can scaffold comprehension using several pathways:

- **Multimodal Cues:** Anchoring words to physical movement and environmental triggers.
- **Interactive Scaffolding:** Designing custom digital assets (like H5P) that let students discover syntax inductively.
- **Relational Safety:** Creating spaces where students are not afraid of making mistakes, keeping their anxiety low.

By focusing on acquisition rather than declarative rule-learning, language ceases to be a subject and becomes a living bridge of connection.
