Language is not merely a symbolic code stored in isolated cerebral modules; it is an embodied act performed in physical and social space. In this article, we examine how **4E Cognition** (Embodied, Embedded, Enacted, Extended) can be operationalized within IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) language units.
